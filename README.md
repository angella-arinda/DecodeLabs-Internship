### 🔹 Project 2: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
* **Objective:** Compute descriptive statistics, spot outliers, and identify transaction trends.
* **Key Findings:** 
  * Average order quantity: 2.93 items per cart.
  * Mean Total Price ($925.44) vs. Median ($548.10), showing a right-skewed revenue distribution.
  * Identified peak revenue outlier (`ORD200010` valued at $3,129.85).
* **Deliverables:** `Project_2_EDA_Analysis-finshed1.xlsx`, `Project_2_EDA_Final_Report-1.docx`
